# Reference research

I inspected the public [Broadway Pizza Pakistan ordering website](https://www.broadwaypizza.com.pk/) on 22 November 2026 from a signed-out desktop browser. The brief prefers one reference site when it supports three distinct findings. The observations below describe that session. The business consequences are plausible effects and are unmeasured. The CampusBites changes are planned for later milestones.

## 1. An order prompt interrupts initial browsing

- **Task** — Look through the menu before choosing how to order.
- **Steps and observation** — Open the homepage in a fresh session. A delivery or pickup prompt covers the menu and blurs the page behind it. Closing it allows browsing without making either choice.
- **Possible consequence** — A first-time customer may leave if they think they must choose a fulfilment method before seeing the menu.
- **CampusBites improvement** — Let users browse from the homepage and open item details immediately. Keep the delivery area visible while browsing and collect the address at checkout.
- **Evidence** — [Opening order prompt](evidence/reference/broadway-order-prompt.jpg).

## 2. The search filters omit dietary suitability

- **Task** — Narrow the menu to vegetarian meals before comparing prices.
- **Steps and observation** — Dismiss the opening prompt and open menu search. Search offers category, deals, availability, and price controls. The filter bar has no vegetarian or other diet control. The displayed result rows show the item name, category and price without an explicit dietary label.
- **Possible consequence** — A vegetarian customer may need to check items individually or stop looking when suitability is unclear.
- **CampusBites improvement** — Put a vegetarian-only filter beside search and label vegetarian meals on cards and detail pages.
- **Evidence** — [Search filters and result rows](evidence/reference/broadway-search-filters.jpg).

## 3. A food search includes unrelated dishes from a combined category

- **Task** — Find pasta options without going through unrelated dishes.
- **Steps and observation** — Open menu search with all items selected and enter `pasta`. Five results appear. The results include Oven Baked Sandwiches and Calzones, both displayed under the combined Pasta | Sandwich | Calzone category. The rows offer no description or ingredient text that explains their relevance to the query.
- **Possible consequence** — Customers have to read past unrelated items and may overlook an option they intended to compare.
- **CampusBites improvement** — Search dish names, descriptions, and ingredients. Use a separate category filter, and show a short description on each result to help customers judge relevance.
- **Evidence** — [Results for pasta](evidence/reference/broadway-search-before-details.jpg).
